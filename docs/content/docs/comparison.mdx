Every community platform makes different trade-offs. Here is how Colbe fits in next to the platforms you may already know.

### vs Discord

Discord is a large, general-purpose chat platform used by gaming groups, friends, and creators alike.

* **Similar ideas** - Servers become guilds, with channels and direct messages
* **Focused scope** - Colbe concentrates on guild-based communities instead of trying to be everything
* **Announcements on the community page** - Owners and admins can post updates where members will see them
* **Quick sign-up** - Register with just an email and finish setup in onboarding

### vs Guilded

Guilded was a community chat platform that Roblox retired on December 19, 2025.

* **Same inspiration** - Colbe draws on the guild-based approach that Guilded and Discord popularized
* **Independent** - Colbe is built and run by ColbemLLC
* **Still growing** - We are building out the chat interface, public APIs, and a developer hub

### vs Workplace chat (Slack and similar)

Tools like Slack are built mainly for teams and companies.

* **Made for communities** - Guilds, roles, and announcements are designed for groups of people who want to hang out, not just work
* **Free to use** - Colbe is free today. Paid plans are not available yet, and we will announce them on the [pricing page](/pricing)

### vs Forums

Forums are great for long-form, searchable discussion.

* **Real-time conversation** - Channels and direct messages keep the conversation moving
* **Different strengths** - Colbe is built for chat first. Forums are a better fit for archives of long posts

### What is next

We are still building. On the roadmap: a finished chat interface, public APIs, a developer hub with in-browser coding, and a native Windows app with a web PWA for older hardware. These are planned and not available yet.

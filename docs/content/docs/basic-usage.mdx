Colbe is a community-based platform. This guide covers the basics: signing up, joining a community, and talking with your people.

<Callout type="info">
  The Colbe application is not yet available to download. You can use Colbe from your browser in the meantime.
</Callout>

## Sign Up and Sign In

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create your account

    Register with just your email. After you register, you will be taken to onboarding to finish setting up your account.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Sign in

    Already have an account? [Sign in](/login) with your email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Guilds

A guild is a space for your community. Create a guild to start your own, or join one to take part.

* **Owners and admins** manage the guild and its members.
* **Members** can take part in the guild's channels and read its announcements.

## Channels

Guilds are organized into channels, so every topic has its own place and nothing gets lost. Pick a channel to read and join the conversation.

## Direct Messages

Want to talk away from the main channels? Send a direct message to talk one-on-one with another member.

## Announcements

Owners and admins can post announcements on the community page, so important updates are easy to find.

## Your Settings

Open your settings to update your profile, including your display name.

## Sign Out

When you are done, sign out from your account to end your session on this device.

## Colbe is live

Today is release day, and we could not be more excited to share Colbe with you.

Colbe is a guild-based community platform. It exists so groups of people can come together, talk, and build something that feels like theirs. Getting here took a lot of late nights, a lot of rewrites, and a lot of people who believed in the idea. To everyone who tested, gave feedback, and cheered us on: thank you.

***

## What is in this release

### Guilds

Create a guild, invite your people, and run it with owners and admins.

### Channels and direct messages

Keep conversations organized in channels, or talk one-on-one in direct messages.

### Community page and announcements

Owners and admins can post announcements directly on the community page, so important updates do not get buried.

### Quick sign-up

Register with just your email, then finish setup in onboarding.

### Documentation

Guides and references are available in the [docs](/docs).

***

## What comes next

Release day is the start, not the finish. Here is what we are working on:

* Finishing the chat interface
* Public APIs through featheredleaf
* A developer hub with in-browser coding
* A native Windows app, plus a web PWA for older hardware

We will share each of these here on the blog as they ship.

***

## Thank you

Every guild created, every bug reported, and every kind word makes Colbe better. We are only getting started, and we are glad you are here for it.

[Sign in](/login) or read the [docs](/docs) to get going. Welcome to Colbe.

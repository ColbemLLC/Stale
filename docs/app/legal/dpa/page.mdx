# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

<small>*Last updated: November 23rd, 2026.*</small>

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) (the "Agreement") between \[LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("Colbe," "Processor," "we," "us," or "our") and the organization, guild owner, or other entity that is party to the Agreement ("Controller" or "Customer").

This DPA applies where Colbe processes Personal Data on behalf of Controller in connection with the Colbe platform (the "Services"), including colbe.cc and app.colbe.cc.

## 1. How this DPA fits with our other documents

* The [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) govern your use of the Services.
* The [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) explains how Colbe handles personal data for its own purposes, such as running accounts and keeping the platform secure.
* The [Community Guidelines](/legal/guidelines) set the rules for content and conduct. Enforcing them may involve processing the data described below.
* This DPA applies only where Colbe acts as a processor for a Controller. If these documents conflict on the processing of Personal Data for a Controller, this DPA prevails.

## 2. Definitions

* **"Applicable Data Protection Law"** means all applicable privacy and data protection laws, including (where applicable) the Botswana Data Protection Act, 2018, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) ("GDPR"), UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended ("CCPA/CPRA").
* **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed by Colbe on behalf of Controller.
* **"Processing"** has the meaning set forth under Applicable Data Protection Law.
* **"Subprocessor"** means any third party engaged by Colbe to process Personal Data.
* **"End Users"** means the people who sign up for and use the Services.

## 3. Roles of the Parties

* Controller is the data controller (or business, as defined under CCPA/CPRA).
* Colbe is the data processor (or service provider) for Personal Data it processes on Controller's behalf.
* Colbe processes that Personal Data only on Controller's documented instructions, which include the Agreement and use of the Services.
* Colbe is an independent controller for the limited data it needs to run accounts, secure the platform, and meet legal obligations, as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 4. Scope and Nature of Processing

### 4.1 Subject Matter

Account creation, sign-in, session management, community features (guilds, channels, direct messages, announcements), and related services.

### 4.2 Duration

For the term of the Agreement and until deletion or return of Personal Data in accordance with Section 11.

### 4.3 What we use data for

We use Personal Data to:

* Create and authenticate accounts
* Identify the person who created a session, so app.colbe.cc can decide whether to grant access to the platform
* Keep accounts and communities secure, and detect fraud and abuse
* Operate community features such as guilds, channels, direct messages, and announcements
* Enforce the Terms of Service and Community Guidelines
* Maintain and improve service integrity

We do not sell Personal Data.

### 4.4 Categories of Data Subjects

* Customer personnel
* End Users of the Services

### 4.5 Categories of Personal Data

* Identifiers: name, email address, profile image, user ID
* Authentication data: hashed passwords, and tokens issued by sign-in providers (Google, Apple, GitHub) where you use them
* Session data: session token, IP addresses a session is seen from, and user agent (browser and device information)
* Content you create on the Services, such as messages and announcements

Sensitive personal data is not required for the Services. Please do not submit it unless the Services expressly ask for it.

## 5. Colbe Obligations

Colbe shall:

* Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Controller.
* Ensure personnel authorized to process Personal Data are subject to confidentiality obligations.
* Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data (see Section 8).
* Notify Controller without undue delay upon becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach.
* Assist Controller, taking into account the nature of processing, in responding to data subject requests.
* Assist Controller in meeting obligations related to security, breach notification, impact assessments, and regulatory consultations, where applicable.

## 6. Subprocessors

* Vercel, for hosting and deployment
* Turso, for database hosting

Colbe shall:

* Keep this list current and make it available on request.
* Impose data protection obligations on Subprocessors consistent with this DPA.

Controller may object to a new Subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds within 10 days of notice.

## 7. International Transfers

Where Personal Data is transferred outside the country where it was collected, Colbe shall ensure appropriate safeguards are in place. For transfers from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the Standard Contractual Clauses in Exhibit A apply.

## 8. Security Measures

Colbe maintains security measures that include:

* Encryption of data in transit
* Hashed password storage
* Logical access controls
* Session expiry and revocation
* Secure software development practices

## 9. Audit Rights

Upon reasonable written notice, Controller may request documentation demonstrating Colbe's compliance with this DPA. On-site audits shall be limited to once annually and subject to reasonable confidentiality and security restrictions.

## 10. CCPA/CPRA Terms

* Not sell or share Personal Data.
* Not retain, use, or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business relationship.
* Comply with applicable obligations of a service provider under CCPA/CPRA.

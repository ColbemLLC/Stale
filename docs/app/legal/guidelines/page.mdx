# Community Guidelines

<small>*Last updated: November 21st, 2026.*</small>

These Community Guidelines (“Guidelines”) apply to everyone who uses Colbe, a service provided by ColbemLLC (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). They form part of our Terms of Service. Colbe is a place to chat and build communities, and these Guidelines keep it safe and welcoming for everyone.

## 1. Be Respectful

* Treat other members with respect, including in disagreements.
* Do not harass, bully, threaten, or intimidate anyone.
* Do not post hate speech or discriminate against people based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
* Do not encourage others to harm themselves or anyone else.

## 2. Keep It Safe

* Do not share sexual content involving minors. We report it to the relevant authorities.
* Do not post graphic violence or gore, or glorify it.
* Do not share or sell illegal goods or services.
* Do not share someone’s private information (doxxing), including addresses, phone numbers, or private messages, without their consent.
* Mark or restrict adult content according to the tools and settings available in your community. It is not allowed in spaces open to minors.

## 3. Be Honest

* Do not impersonate other people, communities, or the Company.
* Do not scam, phish, or deceive others to obtain money, credentials, or personal information.
* Do not spread malware, malicious links, or code intended to harm devices or accounts.
* Do not spam, including mass messages, unsolicited promotions, invite flooding, and repeated unwanted contact.

## 4. Protect the Platform

* Do not attempt to access accounts, data, or systems that are not yours.
* Do not abuse, overload, or interfere with the Services, including through bots or automation that is not permitted.
* Do not evade bans, blocks, or other restrictions.
* Report security issues to us instead of exploiting them.

## 5. Respect Intellectual Property

Only share content you have the right to share. Do not post material that infringes copyright, trademarks, or other rights of others.

## 6. Community Owners and Moderators

Owners and moderators may set additional rules for their own guilds. Those rules must not conflict with these Guidelines or our Terms of Service.

Owners and moderators are responsible for the spaces they manage and must not use their role to harass, discriminate against, or exploit members.

## 7. Reporting

If you see content or behavior that breaks these Guidelines, report it using the reporting tools in the Services or contact a community moderator. Do not retaliate against people who report in good faith. Do not file false or abusive reports.

## 8. Enforcement

We may take action when these Guidelines are broken. Depending on severity and history, this may include:

* Removing content
* Issuing a warning
* Restricting features, such as messaging or creating communities
* Temporarily suspending an account
* Permanently banning an account

We may act immediately and without warning for serious violations, such as child safety violations, credible threats, or illegal activity. We may report such activity to law enforcement where required or appropriate.

## 9. Appeals

If you believe we made a mistake, you can appeal an enforcement decision through the contact options on our website. We will review appeals in good faith.

## 10. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these Guidelines from time to time. We will post the updated version with a revised “Last Updated” date. Continued use of the Services means you accept the updated Guidelines.

## 11. Contact Us

If you have questions about these Guidelines, contact ColbemLLC through the contact options on our website.
